[unsplash.com/s/photos/hamburger](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/hamburger) and the related
searches for fries and milkshakes. All are free photos under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Unsplash+ (premium) photos were left out on
purpose. The app loads them from Unsplash's CDN (`images.unsplash.com`) with size parameters
such as `?w=800`.
